# Roadmap

Pre-pitch prototype, November 2, 2026 to January 3, 2027. Colorectal cancer only.

| Weeks | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| 1 to 3 | Drug Body Map: 50 drugs, single-cell hit map, 3D body view, tissue-level baseline |
| 4 to 6 | Slide-to-Sim: TCGA slide regions, cell finder, simple tumor twin, MSI check |
| 7 to 8 | Join both sides through the drug, window gauge, Ask why, final numbers, video |

## Week 1 steps

0. Scaffold the repo
1. Record the machine
2. Python environment
3. Voyager model access
4. Cell types concept, then the cell atlas (metadata only)
5. Drug target concept, then ChEMBL targets for six demo drugs
6. OnSIDES side effects and the 50-drug list (25 tuning, 25 locked)
7. Close the week

## Out of scope until after the pitch

Sol, whole slides, other cancers, survival analysis, public release, and any outreach.
